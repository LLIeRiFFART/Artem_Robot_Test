We build robots so your home cleans itself

Paris studio, Rue de TurbigoWhere every Lunevo is imagined, tested and argued over.

Lunevo started in a Paris kitchen in 2019 with a simple idea: housework is a design problem. Our founders — an aerospace navigation engineer and an industrial designer — built the first prototype to clean around a very stubborn cat.

What we believe

Three rules we never break

Design that earns its place

If a robot sits in your living room, it should look like it belongs there. No blinking toys, no plastic.

Built to be repaired

Every battery, brush motor and wheel is replaceable. Spare parts for eight years, guaranteed.

Privacy by default

Maps and camera images are processed on the robot. Nothing leaves your home unless you say so.
